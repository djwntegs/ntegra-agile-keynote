Wearing different hats
One role. Four leadership modes.
PRODUCT MANAGER
Strategic Leadership
Operating at the strategic level — product vision, stakeholder alignment, investment decisions, and connecting work to organisational objectives.
PRODUCT OWNER
Turning Strategy into Delivery
Managing and refining the backlog, writing user stories, prioritising value, clarifying requirements and accepting completed work each sprint.
SCRUM MASTER
Enabling High Performing Teams
Facilitating all Scrum ceremonies, coaching on agile principles, removing impediments, and creating conditions for teams to become self-organising.
DELIVERY LEAD
Coordinating Successful Delivery
Coordinating workstreams, managing dependencies and risks, engaging stakeholders, and maintaining confidence across delivery.
